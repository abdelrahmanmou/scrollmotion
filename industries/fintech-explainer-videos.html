Why FinTech Companies Need Explainer Videos

Financial technology is powerful but often invisible. APIs, payment rails, KYC flows, and compliance frameworks are complex even for industry insiders. For end users and investors, they can feel completely opaque.

Explainer videos bridge this gap. We help FinTech companies translate technical sophistication into stories that resonate with busy CFOs, skeptical consumers, and growth-focused investors.

The Perfect FinTech Explainer Formula

After producing dozens of FinTech explainers, we've developed a proven formula:

  • Hook (0-5s): Lead with the pain point your audience feels daily
  • Problem (5-20s): Amplify the cost of the status quo
  • Solution (20-50s): Introduce your product as the answer
  • Features (50-70s): Show 2-3 key differentiators visually
  • CTA (70-90s): Clear next step with urgency
