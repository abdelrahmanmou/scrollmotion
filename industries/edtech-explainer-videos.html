Educational technology serves multiple stakeholders with different needs. Teachers need to see time savings, administrators need ROI, students need engagement, and parents need trust.

EdTech Video Best Practices

  • Multi-Audience Versions: Student, teacher, and admin cuts
  • Outcome Focus: Lead with learning outcomes, not features
  • Engagement Metrics: Show time-on-task and completion data
  • Social Proof: Include student and teacher testimonials
  • Accessibility: Full captions and audio descriptions
