How We Work

Our proven 5-step process delivers high-converting videos on time and on budget. No surprises, just results.

🎯 Discovery Call

We start with a 30-minute call to understand your product, target audience, and business goals. No sales pitch—just genuine conversation about how video can help you grow.

Duration: 30 minutes
1

📝 Strategy & Script

Based on our discovery, we develop a video strategy and craft a script that hooks viewers in 3 seconds, explains your value in 60, and converts them by the end. You review and approve before we design anything.

Duration: 3-5 days
2

🎨 Storyboard & Design

We create visual blueprints showing every scene, plus high-fidelity style frames that define your video's look. You'll see exactly what the final product will look like before animation begins.

Duration: 5-7 days
3

🎬 Animation & Production

Our team brings your video to life with smooth 2D or 3D animation, professional voiceover, and cinematic sound design. This is where the magic happens.

Duration: 2-3 weeks
4

✅ Review & Delivery

You receive the first draft for review. We offer up to 3 rounds of revisions included. Once approved, you get all file formats optimized for web, social, and ads.

Duration: 3-5 days
5
