How to Create the Perfect SaaS Explainer Video (Step-by-Step)

By Abdelrahman • 8 min read

As a Lead Project Manager at Scroll Studio, I live in timelines. I have seen the smooth successes and the nightmares that drag on for six months.

This guide is your roadmap. We are going to break down exactly what a SaaS explainer video is and the exact step-by-step process we use.

What is a SaaS Explainer Video?

It is not a tutorial. It is your elevator pitch on steroids. Its job is not to teach the user how to use the software, but to convince them that they need it.

Step 1: The Script (The Blueprint)

We cannot animate a bad script into a good video. Use the "Problem -> Solution -> Benefit" framework:

  • The Hook (0:00-0:15): Validate the pain.
  • The Pivot (0:15-0:45): Introduce your software as the antidote.
  • The Benefit (0:45-1:15): sell the outcome, not the feature.

Tip: Get final sign-off from all stakeholders before animation starts.

Step 2: Determining the Length

The industry standard sweet spot for a homepage asset is 60 to 90 seconds.

  • Under 60s: Good for social ads.
  • 60-90s: Perfect balance of story and UI.
  • Over 2 mins: Dangerous. Retention plummets unless it's a deep-dive.

Step 3: Storyboards & Style Frames

This is where words turn into images. We create a sketch for every scene (Storyboard) and polished, colored images (Style Frames) to define the look. This is your last "cheap" exit ramp to make changes.

Step 4: Animation & Sound Design

Motion: We add physics, easing, and transitions.
Sound Design: The unsung hero. We layer Voiceover, a Music Bed, and SFX to make the world feel real.

Step 5: The Launch

Don't just upload and pray.

  • Embed "above the fold" on your homepage.
  • Design a custom thumbnail.
  • Add a GIF snippet to your welcome emails.
